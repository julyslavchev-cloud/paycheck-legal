PayCheck – Support

Need help with PayCheck? Write to cleanchat.support@gmail.com. We usually answer within two working days.

Set up Back Tap

1. In PayCheck, open Settings > Back Tap and tap "Add Shortcut".
2. In iPhone Settings, search "Triple Tap", scroll to the bottom and choose "PayCheck ----> CLICK HERE".
3. Tap the back of your iPhone three times after a purchase.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel your subscription in iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new iPhone, tap "Restore" on the PayCheck paywall.

Your data

Everything stays on your iPhone. See the Privacy Policy.
