PayCheck – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

PayCheck is an expense tracker that works entirely on your iPhone. We built it so that your financial data never leaves your device. This policy explains what that means in practice.

The short version

We do not collect, store, sell or share any of your data. We have no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

Data you enter

Expenses, categories, descriptions, monthly payments, your income, daily budget, savings goal and currency are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app's private storage (an App Group shared between the app and its Back Tap shortcut action). We cannot see this data. If you delete the app, the data is deleted with it.

Back Tap and Shortcuts

The optional "Add expense" shortcut runs on your device through Apple's Shortcuts app. The amount, category and description you type are saved directly into the app's local storage. Nothing is sent over the internet.

Category icon suggestions

When you create a category, PayCheck may suggest an emoji. This uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework. The category name is processed on your iPhone and is not sent to us or to any third party.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, PayCheck shows a local confirmation after an expense is saved. These notifications are created on your device. We do not use push notifications and do not receive any device tokens.

Export

The CSV export creates a file on your device. It is only shared if you choose to share it, and only with the app or person you pick.

Subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, email or payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies to these transactions: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Children

PayCheck is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
